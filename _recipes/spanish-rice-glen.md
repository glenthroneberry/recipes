## Instructions

1. Brown rice over medium heat in large skillet for about 10 min.
2. While rice is browning, combine tomato, bouillons, onion, garlic, and 1/2 cup water to blender and puree until smooth.
3. Once rice is golden brown, add 1 cup water, and pureed mixture. I run the puree through a fine mesh strainer and try to get as much liquid out as possible. Place jalepeno in, optional.
4. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 20 min.
5. After 20 min, decide if you need more water for the rice.

## Notes

I do not rinse the rice for this as i have had better results without that.
