## Instructions

1. Put water to boil for noodles and cook per package directions.
2. Season ground beef with salt and pepper.
3. In a large skillet, add the beef and chopped onion and brown. Drain the excess grease.
4. Add tomato sauce, tomato paste, dry seasonings, and Worcestershire sauce
5. Stir well to combine and bring to a boil. Add water and stir well.
6. Reduce heat and simmer for 30 minutes. Add chopped basil before serving if desired.
