## Instructions

1. Bring water to a boil in a pan/pot.
2. Wash and trim woody ends off of asparagus.
3. Boil asparagus for 4-7 min depending on the thickness.
4. Remove asparagus from water and season with salt, pepper, powder, and the juice from the half lemon.
