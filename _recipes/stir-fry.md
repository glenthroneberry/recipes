## Instructions

1. Combine sauce ingredients into a bow and while until combined.
2. Heat large skillet over medium high heat. Add 2 Tbs of oil and cook the protein through.
3. Remove protein from the pan and add vegetables in with 2 Tbs of oil. Cook for 5-7 min until veggies are cooked.
4. Add the protein back in and add the sauce. Allow to bubble, which will activate the thickener.
5. Serve over rice.

## Notes

Substitute different vegetables or protein of your pick.
Could add in sriracha or chili garlic sauce or just serve with.
